Bio

I am a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Hudl, based in Vigo, Spain. I build production computer vision systems for sports analytics, with expertise in multi-object tracking, camera calibration, object detection, and generative models. My work spans model research, deployment, and the pipelines that turn match footage into tracking data.

Previously, I developed sports analytics systems at Statsbomb, researched computer vision at Gradiant, and built machine learning solutions for retail at Desigual.

I hold an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia, with a year at Purdue University, and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of A Coruña.
